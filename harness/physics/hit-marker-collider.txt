# Hit markers have no collider, also when made by a real weapon contact. The marker is made in
# onCreatureHit, which the game raises inside CollisionHandler.OnCollisionEnter (a Unity contact
# callback); there Unity refuses DestroyImmediate, so up to v0.8.7 the marker kept its SphereCollider
# and weapons bumped into it (Windows run 2026-11-23: 113x "Target physics material not found
# [/AchillesTendon.HitMarker]"). hit-npc cannot show it (Creature.Damage runs outside the callback);
# player-stab hits through the game's own weapon collision. Found by the ThroatBalls session.
# Not in the default run (harness/*.txt): real physics on a fallen body, and a stab misses the leg in
# about 1 of 4 runs (3 of 4 PASS, 2026-11-24). Run it alone: tools/harness.sh harness/physics/hit-marker-collider.txt
wait-level
set-option AchillesTendon "Achilles Tendon Enabled" true
set-option AchillesTendon "Instant Death Chance" 0
set-option AchillesTendon "Allowed Damage Types" SlashAndPierce
set-option AchillesTendon "Debug Logging" true
set-option AchillesTendon "Show Hit Markers" true
set-option AchillesTendon "Heel Zone Size" 0.2
set-option AchillesTendon "Max Hit Angle" 180
set-option AchillesTendon "Bleed-Out Enabled" false
player-grab SwordShortCommon
spawn-npc HumanMale distance=2 angle=0 facing=180 brain=HumanDummy
wait 1
# Down it first (a blunt push, no leg hit): a standing NPC's foot is out of the sword's reach.
hit-npc Torso velocity=1.5 push=3
wait 1.5
mark
# Two stabs: the fallen body lies a bit differently each run, and one stab in about five misses.
# Not three: by the third the sword had once stuck in the body and left the hand (run 20261124-092145).
player-stab LeftFoot
wait 1
player-stab LeftFoot
wait 2
# Any leg hit places a marker (green or red); only leg hits log a zone distance.
expect-log zone dist=0.
expect-no-log immediately is not permitted
expect-no-log [/AchillesTendon.HitMarker]
quit
