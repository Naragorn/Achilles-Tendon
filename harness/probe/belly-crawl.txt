# Probe (not in the default run): Belly Crawl Chance 100 % vs 0 %. A belly crawler falls
# forward (the game's face-down stab death, FullBody one-shot) and is held on the
# face-down Crawling clip (never the idle Laying/LayingInjured or CrawlBackwards, which lie
# on the back). Pictures of the fall and the crawl; CRAWL POSE lines (Debug Logging).
wait-level
set-option AchillesTendon "Achilles Tendon Enabled" true
set-option AchillesTendon "Lie Flat Chance" 0
set-option AchillesTendon "Debug Logging" true
set-option AchillesTendon "Bleed-Out Enabled" false
set-option AchillesTendon "Ground Attacks" true
set-option AchillesTendon "Ground Attack Chance" 1
set-option AchillesTendon "Ground Attack Range" 4
set-option AchillesTendon "Ground Attack Interval" 2

# A. Belly.
set-option AchillesTendon "Belly Crawl Chance" 1
spawn-npc HumanMale distance=3 angle=-30 facing=180 brain=HumanMedium
npc-grab SwordShortCommon

hit-npc LeftFoot type=Slash
expect-log | belly (roll
expect-log FALL FORWARD |
