# Probe (not in the default run): does a kneeling NPC move towards the player? Ground
# attacks with a 4 m range (the menu maximum) and 1 s interval log the distance once a second (GROUND
# ATTACK lines). A: kneeler (Kneel Chance 100 %), B: sitting crawler (0 %) for comparison.
wait-level
set-option AchillesTendon "Achilles Tendon Enabled" true
set-option AchillesTendon "Lie Flat Chance" 0
set-option AchillesTendon "Debug Logging" true
set-option AchillesTendon "Bleed-Out Enabled" false
set-option AchillesTendon "Ground Attacks" true
set-option AchillesTendon "Ground Attack Chance" 1
set-option AchillesTendon "Ground Attack Range" 4
set-option AchillesTendon "Ground Attack Interval" 1

set-option AchillesTendon "Kneel Chance" 1
spawn-npc HumanMale distance=3.5 facing=180 brain=HumanMedium
npc-grab SwordShortCommon
wait 0.5
mark
hit-npc LeftFoot type=Slash
expect-log | kneel (roll
wait 15
screenshot a-kneel-15s of=npc distance=3 height=1.5 angle=90
expect-npc state Alive within=0.1
quit
