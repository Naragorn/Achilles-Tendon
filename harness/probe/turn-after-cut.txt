# Probe (2026-11-20): an NPC cut from behind turns about 180 degrees toward the player within a few
# frames (Nadi saw it in the kneel film: "180 Drehung in 1 ms"). TURN lines (Debug Logging) log every
# frame of the 2 s after a player hit in which the NPC's yaw changed by more than 0.5 degrees (2 until v0.8.5), with what
# may be turning it. The second and third NPC are cut right after they spawn: with the first one already
# fighting, they turned to the player within 1 s (run 20261120-101121). Three NPCs with their backs to the player, each in a fresh spot: a blunt hit
# (refused, stays up: the game's own turn), a kneeler, a sitter.
wait-level
# Headset pace (BasHarness 305fce2, its own frame limiter): uncapped, the headless game runs at 330-500 fps,
# and the game's AI turn takes a share of the remaining angle per frame. Runs 20261120-101121 and -101234
# were uncapped; in run 20261120-112206 the older targetFrameRate cap had no effect (XR paces the frames).
